- Daily export (Android): automatically save your notes to a folder you choose, one text file per note, at a time you set
- Add multiple photos to a note, with a fullscreen gallery you can swipe through
- Long-press a photo, or use the red delete button in the gallery, to remove it
- Add several audio recordings to one note; importing audio no longer replaces existing ones
- Each recording has its own player and duration
- Swipe a recording to delete it
- Hide or show the floating buttons from the note menu
- Support for Android 17
